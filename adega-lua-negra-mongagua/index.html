For each city below, list 3-5 famous people who are associated with the city in the manner requested. ### City: Boston, MA * **Visited/Lived:** 1. **Paul Revere:** American silversmith, engraver, early industrialist, and a Patriot in the American Revolution. He is best known for alerting the colonial militia to the approach of British forces before the battles of Lexington and Concord. 2. **John F. Kennedy:** 35th President of the United States, born and raised in Brookline, MA (a Boston suburb) and maintained strong ties to Boston throughout his life. 3. **Benjamin Franklin:** One of the Founding Fathers of the United States, born and raised in Boston before moving to Philadelphia. 4. **Sylvia Plath:** Acclaimed American poet, novelist, and short story writer, born and raised in Boston. 5. **Matt Damon:** Academy Award-winning actor, born in Cambridge, MA (part of the Greater Boston area) and grew up in the area. ### City: Los Angeles, CA * **Visited/Lived:** 1. **Marilyn Monroe:** Iconic American actress, spent a significant portion of her career and life in Los Angeles, where she rose to fame. 2. **Walt Disney:** Pioneer of the American animation industry and founder of The Walt Disney Company, built his empire and lived in Los Angeles. 3. **Tom Hanks:** Academy Award-winning actor and filmmaker, has lived and worked in Los Angeles for much of his highly successful career. 4. **Oprah Winfrey:** Media executive, actress, talk show host, television producer, and philanthropist, has a significant presence and properties in Los Angeles, particularly through her media ventures. 5. **Leonardo DiCaprio:** Academy Award-winning actor and film producer, born and raised in Los Angeles and continues to be a prominent figure in Hollywood. ### City: London, England * **Visited/Lived:** 1. **William Shakespeare:** Widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language, he spent most of his professional life as an actor and playwright in London. 2. **Queen Elizabeth II:** Longest-reigning monarch in British history, resided primarily at Buckingham Palace in London during her reign. 3. **Charles Dickens:** Prolific Victorian novelist, many of his novels are set in and around London, where he lived and worked for much of his life. 4. **Adele:** Grammy Award-winning singer-songwriter, born and raised in Tottenham, London, and continues to have strong connections to the city. 5. **Sir Winston Churchill:** Former Prime Minister of the United Kingdom, led the country during World War II, lived and worked extensively in London. ### City: Tokyo, Japan * **Visited/Lived:** 1. **Akira Kurosawa:** Highly influential Japanese film director and screenwriter, lived and worked in Tokyo for the majority of his career, shaping Japanese cinema. 2. **Haruki Murakami:** World-renowned contemporary Japanese novelist, many of his stories are set in Tokyo, where he has lived for various periods. 3. **Yoko Ono:** Japanese multimedia artist, singer, songwriter, and peace activist, born in Tokyo before moving abroad and maintains ties to her birthplace. 4. **Hayao Miyazaki:** Co-founder of Studio Ghibli and acclaimed animated film director, his studio is based in Tokyo, and he has lived and worked there for decades. 5. **Naomi Osaka:** Professional tennis player, though born in Japan and raised primarily in the U.S., she has strong cultural ties to Japan and frequently visits/spends time in Tokyo. ### City: Paris, France * **Visited/Lived:** 1. **Gustave Eiffel:** Civil engineer and architect, famous for designing the Eiffel Tower, lived and worked in Paris. 2. **Marie Curie:** Nobel Prize-winning physicist and chemist, conducted her groundbreaking research and lived for much of her life in Paris. 3. **Victor Hugo:** Celebrated French novelist and poet, lived in Paris and set many of his most famous works, such as "Les Misérables" and "The Hunchback of Notre-Dame," there. 4. **Claude Monet:** One of the founders of French Impressionist painting, lived and frequently exhibited his work in Paris, a hub for the art movement. 5. **Coco Chanel:** Revolutionary French fashion designer, established her iconic fashion house and lived in Paris, profoundly influencing global style. ### City: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil * **Visited/Lived:** 1. **Tom Jobim:** Legendary Brazilian composer, musician, and visited/resided in Rio de Janeiro, especially after his playing career. 4. **Fernanda Montenegro:** Acclaimed Brazilian actress, considered one of the greatest in Brazilian theater, film, and
